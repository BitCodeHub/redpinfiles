The Red Pin Files is a YouTube documentary channel about history, true crime, unexplained mysteries and science. Its videos are researched, narrated documentaries built from credited archive photographs, maps and documents.

The Red Pin Files Publisher is the channel owner's private, internal tool that uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and it has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads one finished video per day to the owner's own channel, The Red Pin Files.
  • Sets the title, description (with chapters, sources and photo credits), tags and thumbnail that the owner's production tool prepared.
  • Reads the channel's own basic details to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any other user's YouTube account or data.
  • It does not read comments, analytics or viewer data.
  • It does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
